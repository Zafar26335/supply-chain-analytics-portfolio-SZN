Assumptions, sources and limitations

The 156-week history is synthetic and frozen. Promotions can shift dealer orders rather than increase final consumption. Price is negatively associated with demand but evidence is weak; no pricing action follows. Regression fits only training rows, uses seven drivers, and cannot establish real-company causality. Single-holdout selection can be optimistic. The current control-band breach requires review now.
